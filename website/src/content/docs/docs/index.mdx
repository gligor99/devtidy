DevTidy shows what your developer tooling is storing on disk, explains what
each item is and what removing it costs, and reclaims the space safely. It
ships as a **macOS app** and a **CLI** that share one engine and one history.

A few years of mobile and web development quietly eats 100+ GB: Xcode
DerivedData, simulators, Android system images, Gradle, npm, Yarn, pnpm,
CocoaPods, pub, Cargo and more. Generic disk tools show you _where_ the bytes
are. DevTidy tells you _what they are_ and whether you can let them go.
